---
title: Uninstall
description: Remove dotsync from a machine, leaving your dotfiles exactly as they are.
---

Your dotfiles are ordinary files, so uninstalling dotsync leaves them exactly as they are.

```sh
dotsync agent uninstall
rm -rf ~/.config/dotsync ~/.local/share/dotsync ~/.local/state/dotsync
rm ~/.local/bin/dotsync
```

- Other machines are unaffected and keep syncing with each other.
- `~/.local/state/dotsync` holds your **backups**. Copy out anything you want to keep before deleting it.
- The shared repository stays on your git host until you delete it there.
